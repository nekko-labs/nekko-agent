**[Download the brand pack (zip)](https://nekkoagent.com/brand/nekko-agent-brand-pack.zip)**: every file below as SVG and PNG, the social banner, and the license.

You may use them to refer to Nekko Agent and Nekko Labs without asking, as long as you use them as they are. Our code is MIT licensed, but our names and marks are not: the [trademark policy](https://nekkoagent.com/docs/trademarks) has the full terms.

## Using them

### Which file on which ground

- **Light grounds** (white, pale colours): the lockup, the gradient or plum mark, and Nekko, light.
- **Dark grounds** (black, deep colours, most photos): the white lockup, the white mark, and Nekko, dark.
- **Busy or mid-tone grounds**: put the mark on a plain panel, or use the app icon, which brings its own tile.
- **One colour only**: the black or plum version, or white reversed out of a dark ground.

### Space and size

- Leave clear space around the lockup and the mark of at least half the mark's height on every side. Keep text, edges and other logos out of it.
- Keep the mark at least 16 pixels tall on screen (5 mm in print), and the lockup at least 120 pixels wide (30 mm).
- Nekko is pixel art on a 32 × 32 grid. Scale him by whole steps (32, 64, 128, 256 pixels and so on) so his pixels stay square and sharp.

### Please don't

![Six examples of what not to do, each marked with a red cross. Top row: the mark recoloured green, the mark stretched wide, the mark rotated. Bottom row: the white-faced Nekko on a dark ground, Nekko with a purple face, and the lockup in plum on a plum ground where it almost disappears.](../brand/brand-pack-misuse.png)

- Recolour the mark, or use colours other than the ones in the pack.
- Stretch, squash, skew or rotate anything.
- Swap Nekko's look for the ground: his white face is for light grounds only, his black face for dark ones.
- Give Nekko a face in any other colour (purple, plum, grey), a coloured outline, or a new expression, pose or outfit.
- Put a file on a ground it disappears into.
- Add shadows, glows, outlines or effects, rebuild the lockup with another font, or join our mark to yours.
- Use our mark, icon or Nekko as the logo or icon of your own product or project.

## Nekko, the mascot

Nekko is a small black pixel cat, and a boy: he, him, his. He keeps you company while your agents work. On a dark ground he has a black face with a pale outline; on a light ground, a white face with a plum outline. His eyes and mouth are always as drawn. Personal fan art is welcome if it says it is fan art; for anything else that shows him in a new way, [ask us](mailto:hello@nekkoagent.com).

## Fonts

All free, under the SIL Open Font License, and available from [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) and Google Fonts:

- **[Inter Tight](https://fontsource.org/fonts/inter-tight)**: the website's text, and the NEKKO AGENT wordmark (weight 800, letter spacing 0.14em, all capitals).
- **[Geist](https://fontsource.org/fonts/geist)** and **[Geist Mono](https://fontsource.org/fonts/geist-mono)**: the app's interface and code.
- **[JetBrains Mono](https://fontsource.org/fonts/jetbrains-mono)**: code on the website.

You don't need any of them to use the pack: the wordmark in the lockups is already drawn as shapes.
