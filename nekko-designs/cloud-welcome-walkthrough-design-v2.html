Paying: methods by country, device and account

Where the list comes fromStripe Checkout in subscription mode shows only methods that can renew by themselves, for the payer's country and currency, that our Stripe account has switched on. This screen mirrors that so people know before the browser opens. Assumes the Stripe account belongs to Nekko Labs LLC (US): PayPal, Revolut Pay, Bacs and BECS need an EU, UK or AU account and are left out.
Left out on purposeKonbini, bank transfer and Bancontact only pay one invoice at a time; Afterpay, Affirm, PayPay and Samsung Pay don't do subscriptions; Canadian pre-authorized debit isn't in Checkout's subscription mode.
DeviceApple Pay appears on a Mac, iPhone or iPad (it finishes in Safari with a card in Wallet); Google Pay elsewhere (Chrome with a saved card). Never in India for Google Pay.
AccountAnyone can buy Pro for themselves. Changing an organization's plan is for its owners and admins; Enterprise can also pay by invoice and bank transfer.
