Beginner composer, four ways

Four takes on the empty focused-view chat for someone writing their first prompt. Each one replaces what ChatPane shows while cleanStart is on, and goes away when the first message is sent. Everything here is clickable. Switch the theme, or show light and dark side by side.

Recommendation

Ship A as the clean-start shell for everyone, since it is a restyle of what is already there. Put C’s facet chips and coach under a beginnerMode setting that onboarding turns on and that switches itself off after a few good prompts. Use D once, for the very first chat after the onboarding wizard’s Done step. Keep B as a stretch goal: it teaches structure best but costs the most and gets in an expert’s way.
