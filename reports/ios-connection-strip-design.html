iPhone connection strip

One slim row under the title shows every paired computer with a dot and its name. Healthy computers say nothing more. A problem adds one word; tapping it opens one line and the fix under the row. The three stacked banners (connection, other computers, protocol) are gone from the chat list.

Rules

PartSpec
RowUnder the large title, above the list. 28pt items, 8pt padding, 4pt gap, 12pt gutter so the first item's text lines up with the 20pt page gutter. Scrolls sideways when the names don't fit. Shown whenever a computer is paired. No background, no border.
Item8pt dot, 6pt gap, name 12/500 inkSoft. Healthy: nothing else. A problem adds one word in the state colour and a small chevron.
DotsOnline: success. Connecting: hollow ring in running (no word until it fails). Offline: warning. Refused or pairing key mismatch: danger.
TailscaleWhen a computer routed over Tailscale can't connect and Tailscale is off on the phone, a leading item with the network glyph and Tailscale off. When Tailscale is on but the computer is silent, the word moves to the computer (no answer).
OpenTapping an item with a problem selects it (wash pill slides to it, 200ms) and opens one line under the row (height opens, text fades, 200ms). Tap again or tap another to close or switch. Healthy items filter the list instead.
DetailOne sentence in 13 inkSoft, an optional tail in inkFaint (the outbox count). Then text actions: the fix in the accent, the rest in ink; ⋯ with the safety exits for the active computer.
Wordsoffline · refused · no answer · not found · update · key changed. Full sentences live only in the opened line.
Other screensFiles and Monitors show only the active computer's item, and only when it has a problem. Settings keeps the version line as one quiet row.
