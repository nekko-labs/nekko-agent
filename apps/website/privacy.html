Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-13 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what happens to your details when you visit this website, join the Nekko Agent beta, or use a Nekko Cloud account. The Nekko Agent app itself runs on your own computer, and your local chats never leave it.

Who we are

Nekko Agent is made by Nekko Labs LLC ("we", "us"), a Wyoming company at 30 North Gould Street, Suite N, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. We decide what is collected and how it is used. You can reach us at hello@nekkoagent.com.

Visiting this website

This site sets no cookies and loads no trackers or ads. Its fonts and files come from nekkoagent.com itself, so opening a page contacts no one else. The exceptions are the status page, where your browser asks our API and Relay (on Fly.io) whether they are up, and the pricing page, where it asks our API for the current price. Those requests carry no details about you beyond what any web request does, and the API and Relay don't log IP addresses. Like any website, our host (Vercel) keeps short-lived request logs, which include your IP address, to serve the site and keep it secure.

We use PostHog to count page views and clicks on the buttons and links on this site, so we can see which parts help and which do not. Nothing is stored in your browser (no cookies, no local storage), there are no session recordings and no profile is built about you, and PostHog is told to discard your IP address before saving the event. It is loaded from PostHog's US cloud, so visiting a page on nekkoagent.com makes a request there.

We also use Vercel Web Analytics, our host's own page counter, to count page views. Its script is served from nekkoagent.com itself, it stores nothing in your browser (no cookies, no local storage), and it does not identify you: Vercel tells visits apart with a hash of the request that it discards after 24 hours.

What the beta form stores

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, only if you choose to give it.
  • Where you signed up: the page path (for example /) and any campaign tags in the link that brought you (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content). Never the full web address.
  • When you agreed to hear from us, which version of this policy you agreed to, whether you ticked Also send me product updates, and whether you have confirmed, been invited, joined or unsubscribed.

We don't store your IP address with your sign-up, and the form keeps nothing else.

Nekko Cloud accounts

If you sign in to Nekko Cloud (for phone access, sync or cloud machines), we keep:

  • Your account: your email address, your sign-in identity and your plan.
  • Access tokens for your devices, stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Your computers and cloud machines: the names you give them, the region you choose for a cloud machine, and how much they are used.
  • Synced chats, if you turn sync on. Your devices encrypt them before they leave, so we store only a locked copy that we can't read.
  • Feedback you choose to send us, including a name or email only if you optionally provide it for feedback follow-up. These details are stored with your note; they do not subscribe you to marketing, and we don’t spam.

The Relay connects your phone to your computer. Your chats and commands pass through it end-to-end encrypted, so it sees only ciphertext, plus connection details (IP addresses, timing and sizes) and your phone's notification token. It keeps these in memory only. One exception: the short status you see on your phone's Lock Screen (on iPhone, the Live Activity card, at the detail level you choose) passes through the Relay and Apple's and Google's push services as plain text, because they deliver it to your phone for display.

The API keeps no IP addresses in its logs, and no links that contain a token (such as confirm and unsubscribe links).

Why we keep it

  • To check that it really was you who signed up (we email you a confirm link).
  • To send your beta invite.
  • To send occasional product updates, only if you ticked the separate box. Without it, you get beta emails only.
  • To run Nekko Cloud for you: sign-in, phone access, sync and cloud machines.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns bring people to the beta, as totals.

For the beta and product updates, our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or emailing us. For a Nekko Cloud account, it is providing the service you asked for. Keeping our services secure (for example the website's request logs) is in our legitimate interest. We never sell or share your details, and we don't use them for advertising.

Who handles it, and where

These providers process data on our behalf and only to run these services:

  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps its request logs. Vercel is a US company and serves the site from locations around the world.
  • PostHog receives the page-view and click counts from this website, with your IP address discarded. PostHog Inc. is a US company; we use its US cloud.
  • Fly.io runs the API that receives the form and runs Nekko Cloud, in Virginia, United States (region iad), and the Relay, in Tokyo, Japan (region nrt). A cloud machine runs in the Fly.io region you choose.
  • Neon holds the database, in the United States (AWS us-east-1).
  • Resend sends our emails, so it handles your email address when we write to you. It keeps its records in the United States.
  • Supabase handles sign-in for Nekko Cloud accounts, so it holds your email address and sign-in identity. Supabase is a US company.
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) deliver notifications and the Lock Screen status to your phone, if you turn them on. Both are US companies.

If you live in the EU or the UK, your details are sent to and kept in the United States and Japan, where privacy laws differ from yours. Those transfers rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK's addendum to them) in each provider's data processing terms.

How long we keep it

  • If you never confirm your sign-up, we delete it after 30 days.
  • If you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you straight away and delete your details. We keep only a scrambled (one-way hashed) copy of your email address, so we can make sure we never email it again. To be taken off that list too, email us.
  • A confirmed beta sign-up is kept until the beta ends or you unsubscribe, whichever comes first, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • A Nekko Cloud account is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account removes the account, its tokens, its computers and cloud machines, your synced data and your sign-in identity.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • show you what we hold about you (access),
  • correct it (rectification),
  • delete it (erasure),
  • stop using it for now while a question about it is settled (restriction),
  • give you a copy in a common format you can take elsewhere (portability),
  • stop using it for something you object to (objection), and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before.

Email hello@nekkoagent.com from the address you signed up with and we'll answer within a month. Every email we send also has a one-click unsubscribe link.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the EU, the one where you live or work; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; and in Japan, the Personal Information Protection Commission.

Minimum age

You must be 16 or older to join the beta or open a Nekko Cloud account. If we learn that someone younger has signed up, we delete their details.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the version date at the top. We record which version you agreed to when you sign up.
