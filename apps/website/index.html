Your own model server, built in

Nekko comes with its own engine, so running AI on your computer takes a few clicks rather than a weekend of setup. Find a model, see whether it fits your machine, and start using it.

  • Search and download models from Hugging Face without leaving the app
  • See which models fit your graphics card before you download them
  • Load, unload, or keep a model ready so your first reply comes back fast
  • Serve text and image models on one address that your other apps can use too
  • Already using Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM? Nekko finds them and manages their models
  • Add ChatGPT, Claude, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible service alongside

Work and personal, kept apart

Profiles give each part of your life its own Nekko under one account. Switch in a click, and the agents in the profile you left keep working.

  • Each profile has its own chats, saved notes and settings
  • Its own connectors and sign-ins, so your work GitHub never meets your personal Gmail
  • Its own folders, repositories and permission rules
  • A model in one profile cannot read another profile’s chats, notes or connectors
  • Only you can move a chat across. Agents have no tool for it
