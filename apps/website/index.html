WHY NEKKO · EIGHT REASONS

What you can do with Nekko

A model is the AI that does the thinking. Local means it runs on your own computer instead of someone else's. Nekko makes local fast, then lets you mix in everything else.

01 · SPEED

A model server built to be quick

Nekko runs models with llama.cpp under a Rust core that tunes it for the way agents actually work. On the same model file and the same machine it decoded at 154 tokens a second against Ollama's 115 and LM Studio's 116, ran code edits about 4.5x faster, and came back to a paused chat about 40% faster than Ollama.

Measured 30 September 2026 on an NVIDIA RTX 5090 with Gemma 4 12B. Your hardware will differ, so the benchmark harness is open for you to run.

See every number, wins and losses →
02 · ONE APP

Run models and build with them in one place

No more juggling a model runner in one window and your workspace in another. Pick a model, download it, load it and start working, all inside Nekko. Already set up with Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM? Nekko finds them and puts them to work too.

What the engine does ↓
03 · EVERY MODEL

Your favourite models, side by side

Sign in with your ChatGPT plan, add Claude with an API key, or open up hundreds of models, Gemini included, through OpenRouter. Run them next to your local models, pick one per chat or workflow, and hand smaller jobs to the cheaper one.

04 · YOUR MONEY

See what you spend, and what you save

Nekko tracks what your online models cost and works out what the same work would have cost in the cloud when a local model did it instead. Live monitors show your CPU, memory, graphics card and loaded models, so you always know what is running while your agents work.

05 · AUTOMATIONS

Agents that clock in on their own

Check a price every morning, sum up your notifications before you sit down, or review every pull request the moment it opens. Schedule an agent to run on a routine, or have it wake up when something happens, like a new GitHub pull request.

Scheduled runs need your computer and Nekko to be on.

06 · VOICE

Talk instead of type

Tap the mic, talk, and Nekko turns your voice into text with Whisper running on your own computer. No cloud service ever hears it, and it works from the phone app too: your words travel encrypted to your computer and are transcribed there.

Hands-free control of your computer by voice is coming next. See the plan.

07 · ANY TOOL

Skip the window, keep the power

Prefer the terminal? Use the Nekko CLI and MCP server directly, or from another agent. Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can hand work to your local models through Nekko, so a quick review or a big refactor runs on your hardware instead of your token budget.

Connect your coding tools ↗
08 · ON THE GO Beta

Your agents, in your pocket

Native iOS and Android apps let you start chats, approve risky commands and check on long runs from your phone. Everything still runs on your computer, and the connection is end-to-end encrypted.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Share only what you choose

Letting an AI read a prompt is one thing. Handing it your files, your terminal, your microphone and your network is another. Nekko keeps every one of those behind a control you set, and shows you exactly what it did.

01

Choose the information it uses

Context means the information sent with your request. Check what is included and leave out what is not needed.

  1. Context Inspector See the files, instructions, and saved notes included in your next request. Toggle or pin them and see how much space they take up, measured in tokens, the small pieces of text AI models process.
  2. Find relevant files Search across the project folders you choose, so Nekko can find useful code and notes without sending every file to the AI.
  3. Guidelines and memory Give Nekko project instructions through AGENTS.md or CLAUDE.md, and keep saved notes for your preferences. Turn individual instructions and notes on or off.
  4. Save the plan Turn the conversation into a project description in SPEC.md, plus a plan and task list that can guide later work.
  5. Improve your request Get suggestions for making your instructions clearer before you send them. This draft check runs on your computer without an online AI service.
02

Give it useful tools

Connect the tools and repeatable steps your work needs, rather than copying everything into a chat.

  1. Split up a task Delegate smaller pieces of work to separate AI sessions, called sub-agents. Each gets its own instructions, with limits on further delegation and a view of the work in progress.
  2. Reusable instructions Not sure which skill to reach for? Nekko's library sorts skills by the job they do, from research and planning to code review and testing, and shows every step before you run one. Install more from the marketplace in a click.
  3. Connect coding tools MCP is the common language AI tools use to talk to each other. Nekko can use other MCP servers or act as one, so Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or OpenClaw can pass work to your local models through it.
  4. Connect your services Bring in what matters from GitHub, Linear, Slack, Discord, Gmail and Drive instead of pasting it by hand. Each connection is one you switch on, and it only reaches the network when you use it.
  5. Longer tasks with limits Give Nekko a goal to plan and work through. Set limits on time or steps, and get a request for guidance if it stops making progress.
03

Review and correct changes

AI can make mistakes. Approval settings, file limits, and change review help you stay involved.

  1. Command approvals Choose which terminal commands are allowed, need approval, or are blocked. The app checks for risky operations such as deleting files or changing system permissions.
  2. Limit file access Nekko only touches the folders you pick. Your microphone is only used when you start dictating, and speech is turned into text on your computer. For extra peace of mind, Docker can wall off terminal commands entirely.
  3. Review file changes See edits line by line in the Changes panel. Keep or undo individual lines, whole files, or all changes from the app.
  4. Try again from an earlier step If an AI returns an empty reply, Nekko retries and then reports the problem. Use "Reset here" to return to an earlier message and try a different approach.
