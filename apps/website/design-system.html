Quiet surfaces

How Nekko Agent looks and moves. Everything on this page is live: the colours, sizes and timings are read from the app's own stylesheet, so what you see is what ships.

InputButtons are 28 tall for a pointer and 44 under a finger.

Principles

  1. Space before lines.Gaps and type separate things. A border is the last resort.
  2. Say it once.One short line of help, at most. Detail waits behind a tap, a disclosure or a tooltip.
  3. Only what matters now.What does not apply is gone, not greyed out.
  4. Colour means something.Plum for intent, state colours for state, Petrol for information. Neutrals do the structure.
  5. Every action moves.Each change animates, briefly and quietly. Nothing bounces.
  6. One surface per layer.No card in a card. A popover is the only thing that floats.

Separating things

Reach for these in order and stop at the first that works: whitespace, size and weight, an ink tier, and last, one hairline.

Spacing: 8 and 20

8px steps inside components, 20px steps for layout. 4px only for things that belong together.

Rows are 40 tall on a page and 32 in a popover or menu; fields 32; popovers 400 wide; the content column about 720.

Type

Geist for the interface, Geist Mono for code, paths and numbers in tables. One size per job, sentence case, never tracked caps, never a third line on a row.

Colour

A component reads a token, never a hex value. Select a colour to copy its token.

Agent status

Agents show their status with our glyphs, never a generic dot. In a list it is the glyph; on a window it is Nekko with a badge.

Icons

Control icons are Gentleface, line at 16px: ink-soft at rest, ink on hover, accent only when the control is on. In the app each plays its own motion once on hover, focus or press, then rests.

Motion

Every action answers with motion: short, decelerating, never bouncing. One curve, 8px of travel at most, exits about 25% faster than entrances. Try each one.

Detail waits behind a tap, then opens in place, without pushing anything else around more than it must.

Rows and controls

A row has no border and no resting background: a leading glyph, a title, one line of detail, metadata on the right, and actions that stay faint until hover.

Copy

  1. One line per group, at most.Caveats go in a tooltip or the docs.
  2. Name things by what people know them as."Acme · Team", not an internal id.
  3. Never put a secret, key or token on screen.
