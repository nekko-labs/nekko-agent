In short: **use our names and the [brand pack](https://nekkoagent.com/docs/brand) to talk about Nekko Agent, and do not use them to make something else look like Nekko Agent or like it comes from us.**

## What MIT does and does not cover

The MIT license lets you use, change and share the **code**. It does not grant any right in our names or marks: a software license is not a trademark license.

The names and the brand assets are **not** licensed under MIT. This holds even though some source files draw them: the code in files like `apps/desktop/scripts/icon-art.cjs`, `BrandMark.tsx`, `PixelCat.swift` and `scripts/make-brand-kit.mjs` stays MIT, and you may run and change it, but the marks it draws stay ours, and a build you give to others must not carry them (see [Forks and modified builds](#forks-and-modified-builds)). The brand pack files are licensed only on the terms below.

## Fine without asking

You may use the names, and the brand pack files **as they are**, to refer to Nekko Agent or Nekko Labs. For example:

- articles, reviews, comparisons, blog posts, newsletters and books;
- tutorials, courses, videos, streams, podcasts and conference talks;
- screenshots and recordings of Nekko Agent in use;
- listing Nekko Agent in a directory, app list, "awesome" list or comparison table, with a link to us;
- saying your project works with Nekko Agent: "Acme for Nekko Agent", "works with Nekko Agent", "a plugin for Nekko Agent";
- packaging an **unmodified** official release for a package manager or Linux distribution.

When you do:

- Use the files unchanged. You may scale them, keeping their proportions, and crop the PNGs' empty margins.
- Put each on the ground it is made for (the [brand page](https://nekkoagent.com/docs/brand#which-file-on-which-ground) says which), with clear space around it.
- Write the names as we do: Nekko Agent, Nekko Labs. Nekko the mascot is a he.
- Make it clear you are not Nekko Labs, and that we have not endorsed you, unless we have said so in writing.

Personal, non-commercial **fan art** of Nekko is welcome, as long as it is plainly labelled as fan art and is not used as the branding of a product, project or account.

## Needs our permission first

Write to [hello@nekkoagent.com](mailto:hello@nekkoagent.com) before you:

- change a brand asset: recolour it, redraw it, stretch or rotate it, animate it, add to it or combine it with another mark;
- draw Nekko in new poses, scenes or outfits for anything beyond personal fan art;
- put a brand asset on anything you sell or give away as merchandise (stickers, shirts, mugs);
- use a brand asset in advertising for your own product or service;
- say or suggest that Nekko Labs sponsors, endorses, partners with or certifies you.

## Never

- Use our names or brand assets as, or as part of, the name, logo or icon of your own product, project, company, account or domain. "Nekko Agent Pro", "NekkoAI", a nekko-agent domain or an app icon built on our cat are all out.
- Use anything confusingly similar to our names or marks for software, AI or developer products.
- Use the brand assets in a way that is misleading, disparaging, or suggests Nekko Labs is behind content we did not make.
- Register our names or marks, or anything confusingly similar, as a trademark, domain or social account.

Stating facts about Nekko Agent, including critical ones, is always fine. These rules are about pretending to be us, not about what you say about us.

## Forks and modified builds

MIT lets you fork Nekko Agent, and we are glad when people do. If you share a modified build with anyone else:

- give it a name that is not Nekko Agent and not confusingly similar to it;
- replace the app icon, the cat mark and Nekko the mascot with your own;
- you may say it is "based on Nekko Agent" or "a fork of Nekko Agent", in plain text;
- keep the MIT copyright notice, as the license requires.

Builds you make for yourself or for your own organisation's internal use can keep everything as it is.
