# Sub-agents as a tree and as live cards on the plan, a clear sub-agent chat, and plan checks on their bullets (2026-11-06)

Issue: [#1892](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/1892).

## Intent

Philip saw status said twice: the plan sheet's Verify sub-bullets each had a chip on the right ("Check pending on iOS") repeating the bullet on the left, and each sub-agent row in the agent panel had a hollow bullet on the left and its rocket or check on the right. One place is enough, and it should be the left. A check that was running on a step already marked done did not show as in progress. Sub-agent rows did not read as children of the card above them, and opening a sub-agent looked like opening any other chat. He then asked for the chat's sub-agents to be live cards at the bottom of the plan: each with its status and what it is working on as a one-line title, opening the sub-agent when tapped.

## Behavior

- **Plan checks on the bullet.** No verification chip anywhere on the plan sheet (folded line, step rows, Verify sub-bullets). A step's bullet shows its reported check instead, and a reported check outranks the step's own status:
  - running check (`active`): the working ripple (the green rocket since 2026-11-27), even when the step is done; pause bars when the run was interrupted;
  - `passed`: the green check; `failed`: a red X; `blocked`: an amber warning;
  - `pending` or `unknown`: the step's own mark (ripple while the step is active, check when done, hollow circle when pending).
  - The bullet's label and tooltip carry the check wording and device ("Check pending on iOS / Android"), say it is agent-reported, and give the report's age (stale for waiting reports). With a safe evidence link the bullet is a button that opens it; an unsafe link is never offered. The report's detail line is unchanged.
- **Sub-agent rows as a tree.** `[updated #2573: implemented; hidden-Electron geometry verified (parent icon and tree line both at x=93, previously tree x=78), matched default-theme before/after screenshots captured]` The child rows are indented 15px so the vertical tree line aligns with the center of the parent card's subagent-count icon, rather than its title/status column. Under a parent's card in the agent panel, each sub-agent row has a tree line from the card: a tee for every row, an elbow for the last. The row's status icon (rocket while working, check when done, question when it needs input, warning on error, sleep when watching) sits where the bullet was, before the title; the exception is the done check, which trails the row (after the title and model line) so a finished sub-agent's text starts flush left `[2026-11-25, implemented; unit-tested]`. [#2812](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/2812) adds a quiet second line with the child’s persisted model first and provider second; the full identity remains in the tooltip, never inferred from the parent. Parent and child titles are semibold in the ink colour. Missing model/provider values are omitted.
- **Compact child lists (#2812).** `[implemented; 57 focused component/panel tests, desktop typecheck and 12 hidden-Electron checks passed; matching default-theme desktop, narrow-panel and Settings stills captured and inspected]` Show the first two direct children, preserving their existing order, then **Show more (N)** for the remaining count. Each parent has an independent accordion; **Show less** returns to two. No disclosure for zero, one or two children. Extra children remain mounted for the existing grid-height transition, but are inert and aria-hidden while folded; the button exposes aria-expanded and aria-controls. Existing reduced-motion rules suppress the transition. **Settings → Appearance → Agents wall → Always expand subagents** persists optional `AppSettings.expandSubagents` (absent/false means two rows). Changing it updates mounted lists and determines new lists’ default; per-parent clicks are temporary, not saved. This affects the desktop/shared-web Agents panel, not plan cards or native phone UI. A 17-frame expand/collapse GIF is captured; its timing has not been visually reviewed. Packaged app and other desktop OSs are unverified.
- **Fold a card's sub-agents.** The "N sub-agents" count on an Agents panel card is a button when the card has children. Clicking it hides that chat's whole child list (including **Show more**); clicking again brings it back. The button carries aria-expanded and a "Hide/Show this chat's sub-agents" tooltip, and its click and key presses do not open the chat. The folded chats are remembered per chat in local storage (`nekko.hiddenSubagents`) across restarts. Folding hides rows only: the count, status, and the plan's live sub-agent cards are unchanged, and a working sub-agent still counts toward its parent's Building status. Verified by `WorkspaceCardContext.test.tsx` (button, aria-expanded and tooltip in both states, plain text without a handler) and a hidden-Electron capture of the folded and open card. Not checked in a packaged build.
- **Sub-agent chips.** On the plan sheet, a sub-agent chip leads with its rocket or check instead of a robot icon, and nothing trails its title.
- **Sub-agent cards on the plan.** The chat's direct sub-agents are live cards in one row along the bottom of the plan sheet, folded or open, replacing the open plan's "Running:" chip row and the folded "Sub-agents" count chip. A card shows the agent panel's status glyph (rocket working, question needs you, warning stopped or failed, check done) and the task title and saved short model name together on one slim line (#3548, implemented in the shared renderer). Omit missing models; do not show the provider or subscription. The accessible name retains status and the tooltip retains full task, model and live activity. No extra reads or changes to routing. Cards waiting on you lead, then working, stopped, done; newest first within each. The row scrolls sideways rather than wrapping. Clicking a card opens that sub-agent's chat (on the wall, as a window there). The per-step sub-agent chips inside the open plan stay.
- **Sub-agent chat banner.** A sub-agent's chat has an accent banner across the top of the window: a robot icon, "Sub-agent of" and the parent chat's title, which opens the parent (on the Agents wall, as a window there). With the parent not in the list it reads "Sub-agent of its parent chat". The small "sub-agent" chip in the title strip stays.

## Boundaries

Display only: no change to the saved plan, verification reports, routing, providers, permissions or network access. The iPhone app is unchanged (it still shows its own verification chips).
