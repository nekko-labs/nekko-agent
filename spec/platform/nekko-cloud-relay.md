# Nekko Cloud Relay

Issue: [#3242](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/3242). Decision: [0116](../../docs/decisions/0116-nekko-cloud-relay-is-a-pro-perk-with-signed-tickets.md). Phones: [#3243](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/3243).

## User intent

Philip (2026-11-23): "let's make an upgrade for nekko cloud (we can add to perks): faster device connectivity through nekko cloud relay instead of having to rely on tailscale. The user can select how to connect per device, relay or tailscale. We should also use this for device connectivity updates, syncing, etc."

## What the owner sees

- **Settings > Remote access** has one group, **Phones connect through**, with two ticks: *Tailscale* (free) and *Nekko Cloud Relay* (Pro), either or both. Before remote access is on they are the picks **Turn on remote access** uses (both ticked by default when both can work); after, a tick applies at once, and the last one can't be cleared (*Disable remote access* does that). Ticking *Nekko Cloud Relay* works while Tailscale is ticked but stopped on this computer: the Tailscale route keeps the address it had and comes back when Tailscale does, instead of the whole change failing with *Tailscale isn't connected on this computer*. On Free the relay row offers *Upgrade to Pro* (Stripe Checkout); until the `cloud-relay` switch shows it to the account it reads *Coming soon*. Each ticked route shows its own state (*online*, *connecting*, or why not: sign in again, upgrade).
- The nearest ticked route is the computer's main relay (Tailscale when ticked) and the other is held beside it in the same room, so ticking or unticking keeps every phone that still shares a route with the new set. Dropping a route while phones are paired asks first, since a phone paired only that way can't reach the computer until it pairs again.
- With both ticked, **Pair a device** shows *The next device connects through* with the same two ticks, and every paired device has its own **Connects through** ticks (both is *either way*). Changing them closes a live connection on the route the device lost. *Re-pair* makes a code for the device's current choice.
- On the phones (iPhone and Android, #3346), each computer's page has **Connect through** with the same ticks, limited to the routes its pairing link carried; the phone dials only those. The Chats connection row has no page-wide *Tailscale off* item: a computer only Tailscale reaches (by its routes or the phone's choice) says *Tailscale off* itself, with the fix behind a tap ([iphone-connection-strip](../phone/iphone-connection-strip.md)).
- The owner never sees the relay's address. A relay of the owner's own still sits behind *Use your own relay*, and while it is the main relay the page shows it with *Change* instead of the ticks.

## Rules

- **A perk.** Tickets are issued only to Pro and Team (and team accounts while the switch is `team`). Free gets `402 plan-required` with the plans that include it. A downgrade takes effect within a ticket's life (6 hours).
- **The relay knows no account.** It checks the API's Ed25519 signature itself, so it never calls the API per connection and keeps working while the API and Postgres sleep. A ticket carries an opaque account hash, a device id, the room (agent tickets), the plan and its expiry; never an email or user id.
- **Per-device routes are enforced by the computer**, at HELLO, against the relay the HELLO actually came in on; a device can't claim its route. A pairing code may be made for one route; the device keeps that choice.
- **End-to-end encryption is unchanged** on every route. The relay still sees only ciphertext.
- **The account channel is content-free**: presence (which of the account's devices are online), nudges with a topic (`sync`, `fleet`, `library`, `settings`, `orgs`, `routes`) and sealed device-to-device signals of at most 16 KB. A nudge says where to look, never what changed; synced data still travels sealed through `/v1/sync`.

## Status

- Implemented and tested: Cloud API tickets and the switch (`apps/nekko-agent-api/src/relay.ts`, PGlite tests); relay ticket checks, renewal, expiry, room ownership and `/account` (`apps/relay-server/src/ticket.ts`, `server.ts`, live-socket tests); one vector shared by both.
- Implemented and tested in the engine crates: the same room on several relays (`remote:setRelays`), per-device routes (`remote:setRoute`, `remote:pair` with a route), tickets before every dial and renewed on the open socket (`crates/nekko-remote`); the account channel client and sync nudges (`crates/nekko-cloud/src/relay_channel.rs`). The daemon wiring type-checks; the daemon's own tests were not run here (ONNX Runtime could not be downloaded in the build environment).
- Implemented (desktop renderer, 2026-11-26, #3346): the *Phones connect through* ticks, and the same ticks for the next pairing and each device. Unit-rendered and driven in a synthetic Electron fixture (`connect-through-sandbox.cjs`: turning on through the relay alone, unticking and re-ticking Tailscale, a device untick); not tried in the installed app or against a live relay.
- Implemented (desktop engine, 2026-11-26, #3364; unit tested, not seen against the live relay): computers don't call Nekko Cloud or the relay in step. The 60 s sync loop and 30 s organization heartbeat keep their intervals but start at a random point in their first quarter and vary each wait by 20% either way. Redial after a session ends or fails to open waits 5 s, then 10 s, 20 s... up to 5 min (20% jitter), starting over once a session has held a minute. The account channel's ticket is kept until its `renewAfterSeconds`, not fetched again on every redial (dropped if the relay closes the socket without accepting it). A sync nudge starts its pass 0 to 3 s later, and nudges that arrive meanwhile share that one pass. No visual change.
- Implemented (desktop engine, 2026-11-26, #3404; unit tested against a stub API that counts calls, not seen against the live service): an idle computer's sync pass is one call. The timer pass only pulls (`/v1/sync/pull`); the account and sync state (`/v1/auth/me` and `/v1/sync/state`) are read in full on start, on a `settings` or `orgs` nudge, on a user action, when a pull meets a record under a newer key epoch, after signing in again, and otherwise every 10 minutes (the next pass after that). Without the account channel the pass runs every 60 s; while the channel is up the timer is a 5 minute safety poll, because changes arrive as `sync` and `library` nudges. A pull also runs when the channel reports `ready` (so a nudge missed while it was down is caught) and when the computer wakes from sleep (the loop looks at the clock every 30 s and sees it jump), each 0 to 3 s late so computers don't call in step. A missed nudge costs at most one safety poll; nothing is lost, the data and the cursor live in `/v1/sync`. Phones do not nudge yet, so desktops do not rely on pushes alone. Window focus already asks `cloud:refresh` (a full read, answered from memory if one finished under 5 s ago). Remote account deletion and a key-epoch change that no pull reveals are noticed within about 10 to 15 minutes instead of a minute. No visual change.
- Phones, wording (2026-11-25): the connection row no longer says *Tailscale off* about a computer the phone also has a Nekko Cloud Relay route to ([iphone-connection-strip](../phone/iphone-connection-strip.md)). A phone learns a computer's routes only from a pairing link, so a phone paired before *Also through Nekko Cloud Relay* was turned on has no cloud route until it pairs again.
- Phones (2026-11-26, #3346): per-computer *Connect through* and `denied: route` moving to the next allowed route, on Android (#3347) and iPhone (#3348); unit and simulator/emulator tested, not on a device.
- Implemented and tested (2026-11-26, relay only, not deployed): liveness on `/account` and room sockets (25 s ping, terminated after 55 s of silence, so a half-open socket goes offline as on a clean close), and the internal `GET /presence?acct=...` the Cloud API will read (`Bearer NEKKO_RELAY_PRESENCE_SECRET`, 404 when unset, at most 100 accounts, answers `{"online": {acct: [dev, ...]}}` only). Clients already answer pings (browsers, `URLSession`, OkHttp, tungstenite). The relay learns nothing new: the endpoint reads the account map the channel already holds and returns device ids only (no kind, address or time).
- Fixed (desktop renderer, 2026-11-28): with Tailscale ticked and then stopped on this computer, the *Nekko Cloud Relay* tick failed with *Tailscale isn't connected on this computer*, and Tailscale, the last tick, couldn't be cleared, so the owner could not switch to the relay at all. The tick now keeps the Tailscale route's current address. Unit tested; on Philip's MacBook Pro the same `remote:setRelays` call brought the cloud route online against the live relay (`relays[].via: cloud, online: true`).
- Deployed: as of 2026-11-28 the relay at `nekko-agent-relay.fly.dev` is up and a computer on a Pro account gets a ticket from the API and holds its room there.
- Android, account devices connect without pairing (2026-11-28, #3611; the computer side and the iPhone are separate work, #3610): the phone reads the computers' sealed `reach` records from Nekko Cloud sync and keeps them in their own sealed slot. A record whose room matches a paired computer adds its routes to that computer when the phone dials (in memory only, the saved pairing is not rewritten, *Connect through* still filters them, and a tombstone removes them). A record with no matching pairing shows as a computer from your account, dialed with the account HELLO (`fleet:<this phone's account device id>`, a `fleet` proof with an empty `orgId`, the record's room, secret and pinned host key) and offered no *Forget* or *Sign out*; it goes when the record is tombstoned or the phone signs out. A `fleet-device` or `fleet-epoch` refusal reads the account again and dials once more; every refusal has plain wording. A background loop (`AccountSyncLoop`: on launch, on foreground, every 3 minutes) reads the records while the phone is signed in and joined. JVM unit tested against an in-memory Nekko Cloud and a fake relay, not tried against a live computer or relay. **Not yet reachable in the app:** the Android app has no Nekko Cloud sign-in, so nothing feeds the repository a `reach` list yet (`CloudSync` is not constructed in `NekkoApp`, and there is no Keystore agreement key or sign-in screen); until [#2835](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/2835) lands the app behaves as before. Refusal texts for account computers are English only.
- Not done: the phones' account channel ([#3243](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/3243)); the Cloud API reading `/presence` and fleet presence moving off the 90-second API heartbeat; a free push-only tier.
