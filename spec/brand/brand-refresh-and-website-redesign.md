## Pillar tour replaces the promise panels (2026-11-09)

`[implemented in source; headless checks pass; screenshots in the PR; not deployed]` The `#why-nekko` chapter is rebuilt around the five product pillars Philip set on 2026-11-09 (local first, speed, privacy, orchestration, everywhere), less text and no boxes. Heading "An agent built for local models. Finally.", lede "Private, fast, and on every machine you own." Below it, a numbered vertical list of the five pillars on the left (a tablist) and the active pillar's demo on the right; on phones the demo comes first.

- **The list.** Each pillar is a title; the active one shows one sentence and one link (CLI docs, benchmark method, Privacy Shield, connecting computers, phone beta sign-up) and a thin progress line. Without JavaScript every pillar's text shows.
- **The timer.** The active demo's video is the only clock (2026-11-12). The next pillar becomes active on the video's `ended`, never on a timer of its own, and its demo starts from the top. The progress line is drawn from the video's `currentTime / duration` on each frame, so it stays at 0 while a file loads and can never run ahead of, or behind, the video. The video itself pauses while the mouse is over the tour, while keyboard focus is in it (a mouse click does not hold it), while it is off screen or the tab is hidden, and after the Pause button; resuming continues from the same frame. A pillar the visitor picks (click, arrow keys, Home, End) starts from 0 and plays through the hover or keyboard hold; if a demo ends during a hold it waits on its last frame and the tour moves on once the hold lifts. A look change swaps the file at the same `currentTime` and the line holds its place while the new file loads. Under reduced motion nothing advances, the Pause button is hidden and only posters load. **Enlarge** (2026-11-12): a button beside Pause opens the active demo in the page's lightbox with video controls, starting at the frame the visible video is on; the tour holds while the lightbox is open and carries on from that frame when it closes. It shows under reduced motion too (the lightbox plays only when asked), and without JavaScript it stays hidden. **Double-buffered videos.** `.pillar-media` holds two stacked videos. The visible one plays while the next file loads into the hidden one, and they swap with a 400 ms eased crossfade (the incoming video fades in on top; the outgoing one stays whole under it until the fade ends) only once the hidden one has painted its first frame after `playing`, or, when the tour is held or paused, once its first frame is decoded. Nothing loads into the outgoing video until its own fade-out transition has ended (`transitionend`, not a timer; an early preload showed another demo's frame under the fade), hidden videos are also `visibility: hidden`, and a preloaded video carries no poster, since a poster is a frame from the middle of a demo and must never show for a pillar that is not active; a pillar picked during a fade ends that fade at once. While a demo plays, the next pillar's file preloads into the hidden video, so automatic advances are seamless; a pillar picked out of order loads on demand while the old demo stays visible. A look change loads the other look's file into the hidden video at the same `currentTime` and swaps the same way. Only the videos' sources change; `.pillar-media` and everything around it are never rebuilt. Exactly one video is shown at any time. No video downloads before the tour is first on screen. Earlier a single video swapped its `src` on every change, which blanked the box or showed the poster while the new file downloaded. Earlier the line was an 8 s CSS animation whose end advanced the tour, which let the line and the video drift apart: hovering froze the line while the video ran to its end, a new or reloaded file started late, and a look change restarted the video but not the line.
