# Brand refresh and website redesign (2026-10-18)

`[implemented; icons and docs regenerated with repo tooling; no before/after screenshots yet; beta form not live]` The brand moves from a dark tile to a **gradient**: deep indigo `#453A92` to lavender `#9D8CE6`, left to right. This supersedes the dark-tile and "dark-only" wording under *App icon*, *Marketing website* and *Documentation hub* below.

- **App icon and installers.** The same outline cat mark, now white on a gradient squircle tile with no rim light. `apps/desktop/scripts/icon-art.cjs` is still the one source: `make-icons.cjs` repaints the desktop icons, the NSIS installer bitmaps (gradient panel, white product name) and the phone icons; Android's adaptive-icon background is the gradient and the Expo splash/adaptive colour is `#453A92`. The in-app `BrandMark` is unchanged.
- **Site mark.** `scripts/make-site-icon.cjs` writes `favicon.svg` (the tile) and a new `logo.svg`: the cat in the gradient with no tile, used in the site and docs nav and footer.
- **Two looks, White by default.** *(Superseded 2026-10-22: there are now three looks; Dark was the default until 2026-10-27, Colourful since, see below.)* A sun/moon button switches the homepage between **White** and **Colourful** (lavender ground). The choice is kept in `localStorage` (`nekko-theme`), applied before first paint, and read by the docs pages too; it never leaves the browser. If storage is blocked the choice lasts for that page only. The docs are now light, with a matching colourful variant, instead of dark-only.
- **Homepage redesign.** Handjet and Space Mono accent fonts, retro sticker buttons, a liquid-glass floating nav and lavender/aqua section bands. A dot-matrix pixel wave canvas replaces the starfield: about 20 fps, fixed to the screen and independent of scrolling (2026-10-24: scrolling no longer moves or speeds the wave, and the drift runs 20% slower), paused while the tab is hidden, and held still under reduced motion.
- **Media slots.** The product screenshot and feature GIF slots open in a lightbox (Escape, backdrop or Close shuts it and returns focus). They are placeholders until the real media lands; each empty slot and its lightbox read "Really cool animation coming soon from our marketing team when he's back from PTO!" (2026-10-24).
- **Beta sign-up section.** An email form hosted by an animated Nekko (idle, watching, eager, confused, celebrate). An invalid email shows the confused state. When the form has an endpoint it POSTs `{ email }` as JSON and shows an error on failure. **Planned, not live:** `data-endpoint` is empty, so today it only shows the thank-you card and sends nothing. Wiring it to the signup backend above is separate work.
- **Meet Nekko + beta (2026-10-30).** `[implemented, not live]` The beta band also carries a one-minute illustrated tour video, so the page gains a video without gaining a section. Desktop: the square video on the left; heading, perks and the Nekko-hosted form on the right. Phones: heading, video, perks, form. The video is silent, uses the dark file in Dark and the light file in White and Colourful, and swaps files at the same time position if the look changes. Nothing (poster included) downloads until the section nears the screen; it plays only while at least half of it is visible, and never on its own under reduced motion. When it ends, an overlay offers "Join the beta" (focuses the email field) and "Watch again", and Nekko turns eager with "Liked the tour? Pop your email in!". A caption says it is an illustrated tour and the screenshots show the real app. The form's submit behaviour and empty `data-endpoint` are unchanged.

## Trust-first homepage: slogan and five demo panels (2026-10-22)

`[implemented; before/after screenshots in the PR; demo videos not yet produced]` The homepage leads with the slogan **"The personal, local agent you can trust."** (changed from "Your" on 2026-10-22, see below) and a lede about control: the user chooses the models, chooses what data is used, and sees what is happening. The eight-card "What you can do" grid is replaced by **five promise panels** (`#why-nekko`), modelled on Orca's feature panels: each is one wide card with a copy column (number, headline, paragraph, three points, a link) beside a 16:10 demo slot, sides alternating down the page and stacking media-first on phones.

1. **In control**: model choice per chat, Context Inspector, command approvals and line-by-line review.
2. **No compromise**: terminal power with the polish of a finished app; CLI and MCP for people who prefer the shell.
3. **Fast**: "Fast. Really fast. Maybe the fastest." with a bar chart of decode, code edit and four-at-once tokens/s against Ollama and LM Studio, numbers quoted from [docs/benchmarks.md](../../docs/benchmarks.md) (2026-09-30) with the losses named in the note. The chart is static HTML and CSS (no library) and its three colours pass a colour-blindness check.
4. **Secure**: the Apple-style "even we can't see in" stance: local chats stay on the machine, phone traffic is end-to-end encrypted so the relay carries ciphertext, Privacy Shield and Incognito for cloud models.
5. **Convenient** (Beta chip): the phone app, pairing, approvals by tap, on-phone models.

The demo slots are the existing `.media` lightbox placeholders with a `data-demo` key per panel; Travis is producing the videos, and each drops in as a `<video>` inside its slot. **Demo videos (2026-10-30).** `[implemented, not live]` Panel 01 (control) has its video: a real take recorded unattended with the scripted demo runner (`spec/desktop/presenter-mode.md`), the long wait sped up 4x, about 59 s, 1280x720 H.264 MP4 (about 2 MB, no audio, fast start) with a WebP poster from its last frame. A slot holding a video is 16:9 so nothing is cropped. Panel videos are silent loops that load nothing until they are on screen (`preload="none"`), play only while at least 40% visible, pause when scrolled away, and stay on their poster under reduced motion; the lightbox plays them with controls. One file serves every look for now (a dark-mode take); light takes may follow. The other four slots keep their placeholder until their takes exist. The page title, meta descriptions and Open Graph text carry the slogan; the structured data is unchanged. Everything below the panels (beta, model server, privacy manifest, editions, roadmap, download) is as before.

## Dark by default, a tertiary primary button, GitHub marks (2026-10-22)

`[implemented; before/after screenshots in the PR; saved-theme migration and docs pages deliberately unchanged, see below]`

- **Three looks, Dark first.** The homepage opens in a new **Dark** look (ground `#0d0b18`, white text, lavender and aqua washes turned into glows, pale-lavender sticker outlines and hard shadows). The nav button now cycles **Dark → White → Colourful → Dark**; its icon shows where the next click goes (sun, drop, moon) and its label names both the current and the next look. `data-theme="dark"` is on `<html>` in the markup, so it is dark with no flash and with JavaScript off. A saved `white` or `colour` choice in `localStorage` (`nekko-theme`) replaces it before first paint, so visitors who already picked a look keep it; anyone who never chose now sees Dark. The `<meta name="theme-color">` follows the look.
- **Brighter top bar in Dark.** The liquid-glass nav is lit from within in Dark: a higher white fill, a near-white rim and a stronger sheen, with white link text.
- **Slogan.** "The personal, local agent you can trust." in the hero, `<title>`, meta descriptions, Open Graph and Twitter text.
- **Primary button.** One new tertiary-colour gradient (pink `#ff7eb6`, tangerine `#ff9f6e`, butter `#ffd36b`) replaces the lavender fill in every look, including the colourful one, with dark ink in all of them. A custom download icon (an arrow dropping into a cat-eared tray) sits before the label on the nav button and both download pills, and the two download pills carry a small three-stroke sparkle on their top-right corner that blinks in steps and holds still under reduced motion. The gradient slides on hover.
- **GitHub mark** (Octicons) beside the nav "GitHub" link, the hero "View GitHub repo" button and the footer link.
- **Not changed, on purpose.** The docs pages still read only `colour` from `nekko-theme`, so they stay White (or Colourful) and do not follow Dark yet. `og.png` is unchanged.

## Colourful by default (2026-10-27)

`[implemented]`

- **Colourful first, homepage and docs.** Both now open in the **Colourful** look (lavender `#9d8ce6` ground), set as `data-theme="colour"` on `<html>` in the markup, so there is no flash and it holds with JavaScript off. The nav button cycles **Colourful → Dark → White → Colourful**, the same order as before, starting one step later.
- **Saved choices still win.** A saved `dark` or `white` in `nekko-theme` replaces the default before first paint on the homepage. The docs have no Dark look, so a saved `dark` or `white` shows the light docs there, as before.
- **Lavender as the accent, not the wallpaper.** A page painted lavender end to end read as one flat colour, and long docs pages were tiring to read on it. The homepage ground is now pale lilac (`#f4f1ff`): the hero sits in a lavender wash that fades out below the fold, and the band sections (beta, local models, editions, download) are solid lavender or lavender-into-aqua blocks with white cards, so the page alternates strong and quiet. Cards are plain white, and the pixel wave draws lavender-into-aqua dots as in Dark. The docs keep a lavender top bar, put the page list and "On this page" on pale lilac and the article on a white column.

## One sticker, quieter page, cheaper wave (2026-11-01)

`[implemented; headless Chromium at 1440, 390 and 320px; not checked on a phone]` From the design and motion audits ([#1410](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/1410), [#1411](https://github.com/nekko-labs/nekko-agent/issues/1411)).

- **One sticker.** The sticker (ink outline, hard offset shadow that presses down, the pink-to-butter gradient) stays on the download picker only, as the brand's one loud action. Other primaries (the nav Download, Join the beta, Count me in) are flat ink; "View GitHub repo" and the lightbox Close read as underlined links; the theme and menu buttons lose their offset shadow.
- **Fewer devices.** The section eyebrows are gone except the hero's and the beta card's; the "01 / 02 / 03" numbers on the promise panels, the privacy steps and the getting-started cards are gone (the promise panels keep their names).
- **Contrast.** The benchmark bars for Ollama and LM Studio darken (`#177a89`, `#a0601e`) so their white figures reach 5:1 (they were 3.5 and 3.4).
- **Phone.** At 320px the nav drops its Download button (the hero carries the same one; it used to be clipped) and the download pill drops its version tag. Hover effects apply only where there is hover (no sticky lifts after a tap) and taps show no highlight.
- **The wave costs less.** Panels over the dot field (the nav, glass and cards) are solid, not frosted, so they no longer re-blur on every canvas frame. The wave animates only while the hero is on screen, holding its last frame past it (measured: 0 ms of script in a 3 s idle trace scrolled past the hero, against 335 ms before). Resizing waits 150 ms and ignores a URL bar showing or hiding (height-only changes under 120px). The reveal check reads layout once a frame and stops once everything is revealed.
